About SniffScape

A scent-first solution for dogs home alone.

The problem

Dogs left alone for 6–9 hours get bored, which leads to barking, chewing furniture, and anxiety. Existing options fall short: snuffle mats aren’t safe unsupervised and are emptied in minutes; treat dispensers drop everything from one spot; high-tech consoles are built around buttons and lights, not a dog’s nose.

The solution

SniffScape is a set of hexagon-shaped scent pods you place around your home. Each pod has a chew-proof, food-grade silicone “grass” top and a sealed chamber holding part of your dog’s normal daily kibble. Pods connect to the SniffScape app via Bluetooth and open one at a time at random moments during your chosen hunt window, releasing a faint scent from a refillable wick.

Because the dog never knows which pod opens next or when, it keeps patrolling and sniffing all day instead of finishing a toy in 10 minutes. Pods can be spread across rooms and moved weekly. No extra treats means no weight gain.

Specs

Works for all dog sizes. Each pod is 18cm wide, 6cm tall, with a weighted non-slip base. Battery lasts about two weeks per charge over USB-C. Dishwasher-safe (top rack) when the electronics core is removed.
