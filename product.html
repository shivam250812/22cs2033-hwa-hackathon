Features

  • Chew-proof, food-grade silicone “grass” top — no fabric, no small parts, safe for unsupervised use.
  • Sealed chamber holds part of your dog’s normal daily kibble.
  • Pods connect to the SniffScape app via Bluetooth and open one at a time at random moments during your hunt window.
  • Each pod releases a faint scent from a refillable wick so your dog must sniff it out.
  • Random timing keeps your dog patrolling and sniffing all day instead of finishing a toy in 10 minutes.
  • Spread pods across rooms and move them weekly to keep the hunt new.
  • Uses your dog’s normal daily food — no extra treats, no weight gain.
  • Rechargeable battery, about two weeks per charge, USB-C.

What’s in the box

  • 6 hexagon scent pods (Starter Kit) or 10 pods (Explorer Kit).
  • Refillable scent wick in each pod.
  • USB-C charging cable.
  • Quick-start guide and app setup instructions.

Scent refill packs and expansion packs sold separately.

Specs

  • Dishwasher-safe (top rack) when the electronics core is removed.
  • Connects to the SniffScape app via Bluetooth.
