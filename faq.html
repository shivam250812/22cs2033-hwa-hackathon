Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you pre-order.

When does SniffScape ship? ▶
SniffScape is pre-order only and ships in February 2027. You’ll get an email when your order ships.
Is it safe to leave with my dog unsupervised? ▶
Yes. The tops are chew-proof, food-grade silicone with no fabric and no small parts. The kibble stays in a sealed chamber until the pod opens.
How long does the battery last? ▶
About two weeks per charge. Each pod recharges by USB-C.
Do I have to buy extra treats? ▶
No. SniffScape uses your dog’s normal daily kibble, so there’s no weight gain from extra treats.
Can I move the pods around? ▶
Yes. Spread pods across rooms and move them weekly so the hunt stays new. They’re dishwasher-safe (top rack) once the electronics core is removed.
What’s in the box? ▶
The Starter Kit includes 6 scent pods. The Explorer Kit includes 10 scent pods. Expansion packs add 3 pods each. Scent refill packs include 3 wicks (about 3 months) in Citrus Meadow, Roast Chicken, or Peanut Butter.
What is the coupon code SNIFF10? ▶
SNIFF10 gives 10% off the subtotal at checkout. Any other code shows "Invalid Code". Free shipping applies on orders of $100 or more.
