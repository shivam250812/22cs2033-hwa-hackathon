The problem

Dogs left alone for 6–9 hours get bored — which leads to barking, chewing furniture, and anxiety.

Snuffle mats are what dogs love most, but makers warn they are not safe to leave unsupervised (fabric can be chewed) and dogs empty them in minutes.

Automatic treat dispensers drop everything from one spot and most play sessions last about 10 minutes.

High-tech dog consoles are expensive and built around buttons and lights, not a dog’s nose.

The solution

SniffScape is a set of hexagon-shaped “scent pads” you place around your home.

  • Chew-proof, food-grade silicone “grass” top. No fabric, no small parts, safe for unsupervised use.
  • Sealed chamber inside holds part of your dog’s normal daily kibble.
  • Connects to the SniffScape app via Bluetooth. During hours you choose (e.g. 9am–6pm) they open one at a time at random moments.
  • When a pod opens it releases a faint scent (refillable scent wick) so the dog must sniff it out.
  • Never knowing which pod opens next keeps your dog patrolling and sniffing all day instead of finishing a toy in 10 minutes.
  • Spread pods across rooms and move them weekly so the hunt stays new.
  • Uses your dog’s normal daily food — no extra treats, no weight gain.
  • Rechargeable battery, about 2 weeks per charge, USB-C.
  • Dishwasher-safe (top rack) when the electronics core is removed.
SizeWorks for all dog sizes
Pod18cm wide, 6cm tall, weighted non-slip base

How it works

1

Fill

Add today’s kibble to the pod (up to 1/2 cup each).

2

Place

Put pods around one room or the whole home.

3

Schedule

Set the hunt window in the app.

4

Hunt

Pods open at random times with a scent cue, and your dog sniffs them out all day. You get a notification each time a pod is found.
