How it works

Four simple steps to a calmer dog.

Add today’s kibble to the pod, up to 1/2 cup each.

2

Put pods around one room or the whole home.

3

Pods open at random times with a scent cue, and your dog sniffs them out all day.

How a pod works

Every component designed for safety and all-day engagement.

1. Silicone "grass" top Food-grade, chew-proof silicone flaps. No fabric, no small parts — safe for unsupervised use.
2. Sliding lid with motor Precision gear motor slides the lid open at scheduled times. The lid locks shut until its scheduled time so a dog can’t force it open early.
3. Scent wick Refillable wick releases a faint scent when the lid opens. Lasts ~3 months per wick.
4. Sealed kibble chamber Holds up to ½ cup of your dog's daily kibble. Keeps food fresh and contained until opened.
5. Bluetooth chip Connects to the SniffScape app for scheduling and hunt monitoring.
6. Rechargeable battery (USB-C) ~2 weeks per charge. Charges via USB-C port on the side.
7. Weighted non-slip base Dense weighted bottom with grip pads keeps the pod stable during enthusiastic sniffing.
